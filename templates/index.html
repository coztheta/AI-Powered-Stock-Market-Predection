AI Stock Predictor Help

  1. Use the stock search field at the top of the dashboard and press Enter, or select a stock from the dropdown.
  2. Choose Train Model if the selected stock needs model training, then wait for the result message.
  3. Choose Get AI Prediction to request the forecast.
  4. Review the current price, 30-day prediction, expected return, confidence value, and chart.
  5. Use the sidebar to switch between Dashboard, Markets, AI Predictions, Portfolio, History, and Settings.
